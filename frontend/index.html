The route

Five stops between your drawer and its next life

  1. RegisterList the device, its condition and what should happen to it.
  2. Book pickupGive an address. A slot is assigned automatically.
  3. CollectA collector picks it up from you.
  4. Repair or processA technician fixes it, or a processor refurbishes or dismantles it.
  5. Reused, recycled or returnedYou see the outcome and the full history.
Four roles, one record

Everyone sees only the work that is theirs

Role 01

Customers

Register devices, request pickups, follow progress.

Role 02

Collectors

Pick up devices and deliver repaired ones back to owners.

Role 03

Repair technicians

Fix devices marked for repair, or flag them as unrepairable.

Role 04

Processors

Refurbish for reuse or dismantle for recycling.
